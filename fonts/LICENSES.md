# Hero font licences

All four fonts are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL-1.1). The full licence below applies to every bundled subset. The original copyright notices are reproduced for each family.

Downloaded from the Google Fonts repository on 13 November 2026. These are static WOFF2 subsets containing only the Latin characters and space required for `Hazem Zakaria` and its uppercase display. Kerning is retained; unused glyphs and variation axes are removed. Tektur (candidate A), ExtraBold 800, is the selected hero face. Only that font is requested by the live homepage and 404 page. The other three candidates remain available in the local font preview. There are no external font requests at runtime.
