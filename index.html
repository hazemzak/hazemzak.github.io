Quick answers

Who are you?

I'm Hazem Zakaria, working as 3.141Hz: a creative technologist and projection-mapping artist in Cairo, Egypt. I trained as a civil engineer and hold an MA in 3D Animation for Film & Games from Germany, and I've worked as a 3D artist for HUGO BOSS in Germany and SYMSON in the Netherlands.

Do you do 3D work?

Yes. 3D is where I started: I worked as a 3D artist for HUGO BOSS in Germany and SYMSON in the Netherlands, and I make 3D animation, product renders and real-time 3D in Cinema 4D, Blender and Unreal Engine. I made the 3D for Zaid Khaled x El Waili's Lame and directed Manos's Mali W Malo visualizer, together over a million views.

What kind of shows do you make?

Shows for car and product reveals, brand launches, concerts, weddings and live events: content fitted to the real object from measured geometry, multi-projector blends, laser shows cued to music, and LED-wall content at true pixel scale, all running on one clock.

Where can you do a show?

Anywhere in Egypt: Cairo and Giza, the Red Sea and Sinai resorts (Sharm El-Sheikh, Hurghada, El Gouna, Sahl Hasheesh, Makadi Bay, Marsa Alam, Dahab), Ain Sokhna, the North Coast and El Alamein, Alexandria, Luxor and Aswan. I'm also available for shows across the Middle East and the Gulf.

What tools do you use?

TouchDesigner, Unreal Engine, Resolume, MadMapper, Cinema 4D, Blender, ComfyUI and Python, alongside my own tools for hand tracking, gesture control and projector planning. They're the tools behind my projection-mapping, laser and LED shows and my real-time work.

How do I book a show?

Message me on Instagram (@3.141hz) or LinkedIn with the date, the venue and what you want to light. I make projection-mapping, laser and LED shows for car and product reveals, brand launches, concerts, weddings and live events.
